# Security

Yap MCP runs entirely on your computer: it reads only the audio files you name, opens the Yap dictation history read-only, and makes one network request, the checksum-verified speech model download from Hugging Face.

To report a vulnerability, email hello@yapapp.cc with "Security" in the subject. We answer within two business days and credit reporters who want it. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.
